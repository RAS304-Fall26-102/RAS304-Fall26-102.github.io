## Team Charter

From the Team's Goals which can be reviewed in ["Appendix: Team Organization"](https://ras304-fall26-102.github.io/Appendix/App-Team-Org/), the team's charter is:

> Team 102 strives to produce quality projects, not only developing ideas for the greater good of humanity but also to expand the boundaries of public information; advancing the knowledge of ourselves and others at the same time.

## Product Mission Statement

Our mission statement is:

> Team 102's mission is to expand the boundaries of knowledge through prototyping and assembling new projects.
