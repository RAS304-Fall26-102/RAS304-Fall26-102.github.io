## Team Introduction

Team 102 is designing and building one robotic leg for a six-legged walking robot. The project combines mechanical design, embedded control, sensors, and actuator hardware into a modular system that robotics enthusiasts can understand, modify, and incorporate into larger projects. Four PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano development boards divide the work: the main controller coordinates joint motion and communication, while the other three boards manage the servo, linear actuators, sensors, and limit switches. UART is the team's selected communication approach. The current review documents the design decisions and planned operation; electrical integration and subsystem verification are still in progress.
